## Concept

A quiet, disorienting glimpse of a room that feels like it should mean something. The first crack in the amnesia — small, easy to miss, but the turning point the whole album pivots around.

## Production Notes

- Keep the arrangement genuinely sparse — this is the album's "breather" track; empty space between phrases should feel intentional, not rushed
- Vocal should sit close and dry in the verses; let the wide reverb tail bloom specifically on the chorus's "ember"/"flame" imagery for a physical sense of light catching
- The three chorus repeats can each pull back slightly more instrumentation than the last, so the final "quiet triumph" pass reads as more exposed and vulnerable, not bigger
<!-- SERVICE: suno -->
- If Suno pushes this toward the album's brighter baseline (too glossy/upbeat), pull back "chamber pop" and lean harder on "sparse" and "minimal percussion" in a regen
