## Concept

Alice bolts upright into urgency with no idea why — something is late, something is wrong. The White Rabbit's ticking-clock panic becomes her own racing heartbeat, establishing the album's anxious undercurrent.

## Production Notes

- Vocal urgency should build across the two pre-chorus/chorus cycles — verse 1 anxious, verse 2 slightly more frayed
- The "tick by tick by tick" pre-chorus line works well as a rhythmic/percussive vocal moment — consider syncing to the clock-tick percussion
- Bridge should drop most instrumentation for contrast before the final chorus hits at full density
