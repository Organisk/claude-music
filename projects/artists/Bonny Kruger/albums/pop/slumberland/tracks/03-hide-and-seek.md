## Concept

Alice reframes her panic as a game she can control — "if I hide well enough, nothing can find me, not even the truth." The album's title-defining hook: a bright, catchy surface with real dread underneath.

## Mood & Imagery

Bright, candy-colored hallways; hiding behind curtains that seem to breathe; a sing-song chorus with a held breath underneath.

## Musical Direction

- **Tempo**: ~118 BPM
- **Feel**: Bouncy, hooky, glossy pop chorus
- **Instrumentation**: Glossy synth-pop production, whispered "ready or not" ad-libs, handclap percussion

## Production Notes

- This is the title track and likely lead single — chorus should be the most polished, radio-ready moment on the album
- The whispered "ready or not" ad-libs in pre-chorus and outro should use a consistent pitch/processing treatment (establishes the archetype-voice palette used later in the album)
- Handclaps in the chorus reinforce the "playground game" surface texture

<!-- SERVICE: suno -->

- If the chorus reads too dark/aggressive in generation, pull back "dark pop" and lean harder on "synth-pop, dance-pop" in a regen
