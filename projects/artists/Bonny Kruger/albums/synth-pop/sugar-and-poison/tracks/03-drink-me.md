## Concept

Alice finds the bottle, drinks without thinking, and her body changes size against her will — a metaphor for losing control over your own body/self-image. Verses stay curious and composed (English mask); the bridge is where control fully breaks and the raw Swedish voice cuts through in panic. This is where the listener learns the album's central mechanic: English mask, Swedish raw voice.

## Mood & Imagery

A bottle labeled in careful handwriting, a body that no longer fits the room, walls rushing close or far away.

## Musical Direction

- **Tempo**: ~95–105 BPM
- **Feel**: Curious and composed in verses; bridge drops to distorted/unprocessed vocal with sudden low-end weight
- **Instrumentation**: Glassy bell-synth intro, clean/processed lead vocal (mask), raw unprocessed vocal + heavy sub in the bridge

## Production Notes

- This is the album's MVP/proof-of-concept track — the first real-world test of whether Suno can execute a clean mid-song English→Swedish register switch. Listen specifically for: (1) whether the Bridge actually renders in Swedish rather than reverting to English, (2) whether the vocal texture audibly shifts between mask and raw sections, (3) whether å/ä/ö are pronounced correctly.
- If Suno flattens the mask/raw contrast (both sections sound the same), try regenerating with the Bridge as its own more heavily-tagged section, or consider splitting into two takes and stitching via Cover/Extend.
- If Swedish renders poorly, the fallback is per-word phonetic respelling in the Bridge only — flag which words broke and re-prompt.
- Sample idea: detuned music-box motif for the Outro, reused from the album's general sonic palette (ties to Sugar and Poison's overall production identity, not track-specific).
