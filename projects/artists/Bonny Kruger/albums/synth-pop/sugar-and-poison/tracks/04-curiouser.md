## Concept

Everything is accelerating and stranger, and Alice narrates it with forced composure — "curiouser and curiouser" as the mantra she repeats to convince herself she's fine. This phrase recurs bitterly/ironically in track 11 (The Trial).

## Mood & Imagery

Doors that talk, a garden glimpsed through a keyhole she can't reach, her own voice sounding thinner each time she speaks, a watch ticking faster than it should.

## Musical Direction

- **Tempo**: ~130 BPM
- **Feel**: Anxious uptempo, clipped vocal delivery, layered arps building tension without release
- **Instrumentation**: Layered synth arps, ticking-clock SFX motif, tight clipped drums
