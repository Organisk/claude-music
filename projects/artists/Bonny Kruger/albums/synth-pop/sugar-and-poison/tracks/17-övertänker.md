## Concept

**Bonus track — not part of the album's five-act Alice in Wonderland arc.** No mask/raw-voice (English/Swedish) device, no Alice framing. A standalone confessional track, entirely in Swedish, about intrusive thoughts and compulsive overthinking. The narrator addresses their own "hjärnspöken" (brain ghosts) as a second-person "du" — a companion/tormentor that walks them from daytime rumination into full panic and, by the outro, into a nightmare landscape. The chorus hook ("Jag övertänker" — I overthink) is both the title and the thesis.

It shares the album's "candy-coated darkness" instinct (bright pop-bounce chorus hiding real anguish) but arrives at it through a different mechanism — production contrast (cyberpunk industrial verse vs. K-pop pop-bounce chorus) rather than the album's English-mask/Swedish-voice device.

## Mood & Imagery

- Night falling as the trigger for the spiral ("När natten faller in")
- Voices/thoughts as an external, almost physical presence ("huvudets röster", "hjärnspöken")
- Medication/dopamine as clinical, frozen-in-time imagery ("Medicin och dopamin / Står still i tid")
- Sleep paralysis, buttons sewn over the eyes (deliberate, acknowledged nod to body-horror imagery)
- Closing image: dancing through a thorned rose garden with barbed wire around the throat — beauty and self-harm fused into one waltz

## Musical Direction

- **Tempo**: 124 BPM, constant through the genre switch
- **Feel**: Cold and claustrophobic in verses, exploding into bright maximalist euphoria in the chorus — the production performs the lyric's own "smile outward, war inside" contrast
- **Instrumentation**: Verses — pulsing analog bass, glitchy digital artifacts, sparse cold synth pads, minimal drums. Chorus — glossy maximalist EDM bounce drop, layered vocal harmonies, chant ad-libs, bright synth stabs, four-on-the-floor kick. Bridge — industrial breakdown, distorted low synth.
